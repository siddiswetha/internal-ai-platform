# Travel Expense Policy

For travel expenses, keep a receipt for every cost: transport, hotel, and meals. Submit the receipts with a short note saying the date, the place, and the reason for the trip.

Travel expenses up to $500 can be approved by the team lead. Travel expenses over $500 need approval from a manager before the money is paid back.

Submit expenses within 14 days of the trip. Expenses sent later than 14 days need a note explaining the delay.
