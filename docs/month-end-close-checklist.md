# Month-End Close Checklist

Close the month in this order. First, reconcile the ledger against the bank statement and flag any amount or date differences. Second, check that every invoice for the month is recorded and that no invoice is missing. Third, review the close checklist with a second person before the numbers are locked.

After the close checklist is finished, the finance lead signs off, the monthly report is prepared, and the locked numbers are shared with the team. Do not change locked numbers without a new review and a note explaining the change.

The ledger is the list of what the company thinks it paid. The bank statement is what actually left the account. A match means the same reference, amount, and date. A mismatch means the reference matches but the amount or the date is different.
