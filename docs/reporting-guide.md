# Monthly Reporting Guide

A monthly report should include total spend by department, total spend by category, the top vendors by spend, and a short note on anything unusual. Reports are published once a month, after the month-end close is finished.

Dashboards for monthly reporting are built from the same monthly numbers. A dashboard shows total spend, spend by department, and spend by category, so a reader can see the same figures as the written report without reading every line.

Keep every report simple. Use plain words, show the numbers next to the month they belong to, and say where each number came from.
