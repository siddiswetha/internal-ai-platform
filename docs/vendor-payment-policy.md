# Vendor Payment Policy

To submit a vendor invoice, send the invoice, the purchase order, and the delivery note to the finance team. The finance team checks the amount, the vendor name, and the date before the invoice is approved for payment.

Vendor invoices are paid within 30 days of approval, on the normal payment run. Payments over $1,000 need approval from a manager before they are paid. Payments of $1,000 or less need approval from the team lead.

If an invoice is missing a purchase order, ask the vendor to send it before the invoice is paid. Do not pay an invoice that has no purchase order and no delivery note.
